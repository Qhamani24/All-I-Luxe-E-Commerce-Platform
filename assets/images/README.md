## 🏷️ Asset Naming & Contribution Guidelines

1. **Naming Standard**: Use `kebab-case` or descriptive camelCase:
   - Primary view: `itemName.jpg`
   - Alternate angles: `itemName1.2.jpg`, `itemName1.3.jpg`
2. **Resolution & Sizing**:
   - Product Cards: minimum 600px width.
   - Hero Banners: 1920px × 800px recommended.
3. **Format**:
   - Photographs: High-quality `.jpg` or `.webp` (optimized under 350KB where possible).
   - Logos/Icons: Transparent `.png` or `.svg`.
