Returns Policy

🔄 Eligibility

Items may be returned within 14 days of delivery, provided they are unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.

📦 Return Process

To initiate a return, please contact our Customer Care team. We will provide you with a return authorization and instructions.

💳 Refunds

Refunds are processed within 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Funds will be credited to your original payment method.

🚚 Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was defective or incorrect. We recommend using a tracked courier service.

❗ Non-Returnable Items

Custom-made or clearance items cannot be returned unless faulty. Please review product details carefully before purchase.
